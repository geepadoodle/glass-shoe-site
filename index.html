How it works

Glass Shoe learns the shape of your feet and turns it into a fit you can trust.

  • A % fit score for any shoe

    Every shoe gets a personalized fit rating based on your real measurements.

  • Your true size in every brand

    See the size to order in US, UK, EU, or centimeters — translated per brand.

  • Private by design

    Scans are computed on your device — we store measurements, never camera images, and only with your explicit consent.

Your privacy, your control

You can export or delete everything we hold about you at any time — in the app under Profile → Privacy & data, or from this site.

Delete your account or data

Request account deletion, a data export, or withdrawal of biometric consent. In-app is fastest; a web request form is available too.
