Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-07-30

This policy covers data that state consumer-health-data laws (e.g. Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370) may treat as consumer health data. It is provided as a separate policy, in addition to our general Privacy Policy.

1. Categories of consumer health data we collect, and why

Foot measurements you capture (foot length, width, and instep height in millimeters) and data derived from them (estimated shoe size and foot-shape category). Collected and used for one purpose: computing shoe-fit ratings and size recommendations you request. For the default camera/LiDAR scan, measurements are computed on your device and we do not collect images, video, depth maps, meshes, or 3D models of your feet to our servers; a few scan-review photos are stored only on your device and deleted when you delete your data. The optional "Analyze a photo" feature is the one transmission exception: the single photo you choose is sent to a third-party AI processor to estimate foot shape and is deleted by them within 30 days and never stored on our servers — a copy remains on your device (see §4).

2. Sources

Directly from you: your device's camera/LiDAR scan, your manual measurement entry, your size estimate, or a photo you choose to upload for the optional AI shape analysis. No other sources.

3. Categories of consumer health data we share

None. We do not sell or share your consumer health data. Community statistics (e.g. "feet like yours") are computed only from aggregated, de-identified data that does not identify you.

4. Third parties and affiliates

We do not sell or share your consumer health data, and we have no advertising affiliates. We use processors that act only on our behalf, under contract, to operate the service: our hosting provider stores your measurements; and, only if you use the optional "Analyze a photo" feature, an AI provider (Anthropic) receives that one photo transiently to return a foot-shape estimate and deletes it within 30 days.

5. Your rights and how to exercise them

You may (a) confirm whether we collect your consumer health data and access it — in the app under Profile → Privacy & data → "Export my data (JSON)"; (b) withdraw consent — Profile → Privacy & data → "Withdraw consent & delete my scans" (withdrawal also deletes stored measurements); (c) delete your data — "Delete my data" or "Delete my account" in the same screen, or use our Data & Privacy Rights page, or email turtle91021.gnps@gmail.com. We honor requests within the time your state's law requires; if we refuse, you may appeal by replying "Appeal", and if the appeal is denied you may contact your state Attorney General.

This policy mirrors the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy presented in the Glass Shoe app. If the two ever differ, the version presented in the app at the time of your acceptance governs.
