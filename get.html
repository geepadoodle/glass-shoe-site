Why people use it

  • A % fit score for any shoe

    Every shoe gets a personalized rating based on your real foot measurements.

  • Your true size in every brand

    See the size to actually order — in US, UK, EU, or centimeters, translated per brand.

  • Private by design

    Scans are computed on your device. We store measurements, never camera images, and only with your explicit consent.
